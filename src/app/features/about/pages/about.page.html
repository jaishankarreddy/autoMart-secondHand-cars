Our story

Born in Bengaluru, built on trust

What started as a two-person garage in Bengaluru is now a network of certified vehicles across all 31 districts of Karnataka. We saw how stressful used-vehicle deals were — opaque pricing, surprise repairs and paperwork nightmares — and decided to fix it.

Every car and bike on AutoMart passes a 200-point inspection, comes with transparent pricing and includes complete transfer support, so you can buy with total peace of mind.
